Indonesian Coal

Indonesia is one of the world's established sources of thermal coal, offering a broad range of grades for industrial boilers, process heat, power generation and other energy-intensive applications.

At Kailash Coal & Coke Co. Ltd. (KCCL), we source Indonesian coal through established international supply channels and support customers in identifying suitable grades based on calorific value, moisture, ash, sulphur and specific operational requirements.

Indonesian coal is widely preferred for its availability across multiple calorific ranges, comparatively favourable sulphur characteristics, suitable combustion performance and competitive commercial proposition for bulk industrial consumption.

KCCL combines sourcing experience, commercial understanding, quality coordination and logistics support to provide dependable imported coal solutions for industrial customers across India.

PRODUCT ADVANTAGES

Key Characteristics

Indonesian coal is available in multiple grades, allowing industrial consumers to select a suitable balance of energy output, moisture, ash and commercial value for their process.

Multiple Calorific Ranges

Grades available across varied GAR and NAR requirements for different industrial applications.

Favourable Sulphur Profile

Several Indonesian grades offer comparatively low sulphur characteristics for thermal applications.

Suitable Combustion

Selected grades offer dependable combustion characteristics for boilers and process heating systems.

Bulk Availability

Established Indonesian supply markets support regular procurement and large-volume industrial requirements.

Commercial Flexibility

Multiple coal grades help customers optimise fuel selection according to process and commercial priorities.

Industrial Versatility

Suitable grades can support paper, chemicals, sugar, manufacturing, brick kilns and related industries.
