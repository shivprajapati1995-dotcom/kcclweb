US Origin Coal

The United States has a diverse coal resource base spanning the Appalachian, Interior and Western coal-producing regions, with coal ranks ranging from anthracite and bituminous coal to subbituminous coal and lignite.

At Kailash Coal & Coke Co. Ltd. (KCCL), we support customers seeking US-origin coal by evaluating the required coal rank, application, quality parameters, commercial requirements and logistics before coordinating suitable sourcing options.

US coal is sourced from several distinct producing regions. Appalachian coal is associated largely with bituminous grades and also includes Pennsylvania anthracite, while the Illinois Basin is a major bituminous-coal region. The Powder River Basin in Wyoming and Montana is especially known for large-scale subbituminous coal production.

KCCL's role is to help industrial customers identify the most appropriate US-origin coal solution based on end use, fuel characteristics, cargo availability and delivered commercial value.

PRODUCT ADVANTAGES

Key Characteristics

The broad US coal resource base allows customers to evaluate different coal ranks and producing regions according to heat value, moisture, sulphur, ash, combustion behaviour and application.

Diverse Coal Ranks

US production includes anthracite, bituminous, subbituminous and lignite coal.

Established Mining Regions

Major coal production is concentrated across Appalachian, Interior and Western regions.

Thermal Applications

Multiple US coal ranks are used for power generation and industrial thermal requirements.

Metallurgical Potential

Certain Appalachian bituminous coals possess metallurgical characteristics for steelmaking.

Large-Scale Mining

The Powder River Basin supports some of the largest surface coal mines in the United States.

Specification-Based Sourcing

Coal can be selected according to application, rank and shipment-specific quality requirements.

US COAL REFERENCE

Major US Coal Regions & Coal Ranks

The United States has several distinct coal-producing regions. This table is a sourcing overview, not a guaranteed KCCL cargo specification. Final quality must always be confirmed for the specific mine, supplier and shipment.

Region / Basin Key States Primary Coal Rank General Characteristics Common Applications
Powder River Basin Wyoming, Montana Subbituminous Large-scale surface-mined coal from the Western region; generally associated with lower sulphur and higher moisture than many eastern bituminous coals. Thermal power generation and large utility applications.
Appalachian Region West Virginia, Pennsylvania, Eastern Kentucky, Virginia, Ohio and nearby states Primarily Bituminous Higher-rank coal region with a long history of underground and surface mining. Some Appalachian bituminous coal has metallurgical properties. Thermal, industrial and selected metallurgical applications.
Pennsylvania Anthracite Pennsylvania Anthracite Highest-rank coal, characterised by high carbon content and lower volatile matter compared with lower coal ranks. Specialty heating and selected industrial / carbon applications.
Illinois Basin Illinois, Indiana, Western Kentucky Bituminous Major Interior-region coal source with substantial underground production and established industrial supply. Utility boilers, industrial thermal use and manufacturing.
North Dakota / Gulf Coast Lignite North Dakota, Texas and nearby lignite areas Lignite Lower-rank coal with comparatively higher moisture and lower heat content than bituminous and subbituminous coal. Primarily local power generation and mine-mouth applications.

Important: US coal quality can vary significantly by basin, mine, seam and shipment. KCCL should publish calorific value, ash, sulphur, moisture, volatile matter, HGI and sizing only after confirming the actual cargo specification.
