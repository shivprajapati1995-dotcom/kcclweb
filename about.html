Our Company

Reliable Sourcing. Consistent Supply.
Enduring Partnerships.

Kailash Coal & Coke Co. Ltd. (KCCL) is an established coal trading and supply company serving industrial consumers, coal traders and brick kilns across India. Since 1984, we have built our business around one clear objective: understanding the customer’s fuel requirement and delivering the right coal through reliable sourcing, efficient coordination and dependable supply.

KCCL deals in imported thermal coal from major international coal markets including Indonesia, Australia, South Africa and the United States, along with metallurgical coke for specialised industrial applications.

Our role extends beyond coal trading. We support customers throughout the supply process, from understanding calorific value and technical requirements to selecting a suitable origin and grade, coordinating procurement, reviewing quality parameters, managing port and documentation activities, and planning onward logistics to the required destination.

  • Vision

    To be a trusted and preferred coal supply partner for industries across India by delivering reliable energy solutions, maintaining consistent quality, and building long-term business relationships.

  • Mission

    To source and supply quality domestic and imported coal with efficiency, transparency, and dependable service. We aim to understand every customer’s requirement, ensure timely delivery, strengthen our distribution network, and continuously improve the value we provide to our partners.

OUR HISTORY

A Legacy Built on Trust Since 1984

Our Journey

Established in 1984, Kailash Coal & Coke Co. Ltd. (KCCL) has grown from a regional coal trading company into a trusted name in imported coal sourcing and industrial supply. Over the decades, we have built strong relationships with customers across diverse industries through dependable sourcing, consistent quality, transparent business practices, and reliable supply coordination.

With deep market knowledge and access to major international coal origins including Indonesia, Australia, South Africa, and the United States, KCCL continues to support industries with coal solutions suited to their operational requirements. Our journey is defined by experience, adaptability, and long-term partnerships.

  • Four Decades of Experience

    Industry knowledge built since 1984 through long-standing customer and supplier relationships.

  • Relationships Built to Last

    Trusted by industries, traders and business partners through consistent service and professional commitment.

Kailash Coal & Coke Co. Ltd. is a trusted coal trading and supply company with decades of industry experience. We source and deliver quality coal from India and global markets, backed by reliable logistics, timely supply, and customer-focused service.
