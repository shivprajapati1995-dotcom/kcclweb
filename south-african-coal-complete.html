IMPORTED COAL

South African Coal

South Africa is one of Africa's leading coal-producing countries and has a long-established export industry supported by extensive coalfields, rail infrastructure and the Richards Bay Coal Terminal.

At Kailash Coal & Coke Co. Ltd. (KCCL), we support the sourcing of South African coal for industrial customers seeking established export grades, dependable heat value and suitable fuel characteristics for energy-intensive operations.

South African export coal is widely traded in several calorific ranges, including commonly referenced 6000 NAR, 5500 NAR and 4800 NAR grades. These grades are used across industrial boilers, steel-related applications, cement, manufacturing and other thermal processes.

KCCL combines international sourcing experience with specification review, commercial coordination, port handling and logistics planning to support reliable imported coal supply.
